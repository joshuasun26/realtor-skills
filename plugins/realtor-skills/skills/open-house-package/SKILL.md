# Open House Package — the orchestrator

An open house is a dated event, so this orchestrator is built around a countdown rather
than a checklist. Ask for the date and time window first; everything else schedules off
it.

**Every step is its own skill and runs alone.** An agent who only needs the QR page runs
`open-house-signin`.

---

## The chain

```
open-house-package
├─ 0. agent-profile           (only if profile/AGENT.md is missing)
├─ 1. listing-intake           → listings/<slug>/LISTING.md  (skip if it exists)
├─ 2. open-house-signin        → sign-in page + QR  (do this FIRST, the flyer needs the QR)
├─ 3. open-house-flyer         → event flyer PDF with the QR embedded
├─ 4. listing-carousel (A)     → the promo carousel
├─ 5. buydown-math             → payment scenarios          [may BLOCK]
├─ 6. listing-carousel (B)     → buydown carousel           [skipped if 5 blocked]
├─ 7. social-caption × N       → captions for each post
├─ 8. compliance-check + source-check   → gates
├─ 9. day-of checklist         → produced here
└─ 10. open-house-followup     → AFTER the event, from the sign-in data
```

Step 2 runs before step 3. The flyer embeds the QR, and building the flyer first means
rebuilding it.

## The countdown

| When | What | Skill |
|---|---|---|
| T-7 days | Sign-in page live, QR tested on a real phone | `open-house-signin` |
| T-6 days | Flyers printed. Order more than you think. | `open-house-flyer` |
| T-4 days | Listing carousel posts | `listing-carousel` A |
| T-3 days | Buydown carousel posts | `listing-carousel` B |
| T-2 days | Neighbor invitation delivered | below |
| T-1 day | Day-of checklist reviewed, signs and batteries checked | below |
| Day of | Run it | — |
| T+6 hours | Message 1 to everyone who signed in | `open-house-followup` |
| T+3 days | Message 2 | `open-house-followup` |
| T+7 days | Message 3, then long cadence | `followup-sequence` |

**Post the listing carousel and the buydown carousel on different days.** They compete.

## The neighbor invitation

The highest-value and most-skipped piece. Neighbors are the future sellers on that street,
and they come to open houses to see what their own home is worth.

Produce a short printed invitation for the surrounding homes — the property, the day and
time, and a genuine "come see what your neighbor's place looks like inside." No valuation
claim, no "find out what your home is worth" hook that implies a number.

Check the agent's state and local rules on unaddressed mail drops and do-not-solicit
lists before suggesting a walk. Where solicitation restrictions apply, this is a hand
delivery to doors, not a mailer, and the agent decides.

## Day-of checklist (produce this, agents actually use it)

Signs and directional arrows with the count needed. Sign-in QR card by the door plus a
paper backup. Printed flyers, plus the buydown flyer if it exists. Business cards. Phone
charger. Something to write notes on — **the notes from the day are what make the
follow-up worth anything**, and they are always the thing that gets skipped. Lights on,
blinds open, thermostat set, valuables and medications secured, pets handled.

One line to say to every visitor: ask what brought them by. That answer is the whole
segment field on the sign-in.

## After

Do not consider this run complete when the flyers are printed. Set the expectation
explicitly: **the follow-up is the deliverable, the open house is the event.** Tell the
agent when to come back and run step 10, and what to bring (their notes from the day).
