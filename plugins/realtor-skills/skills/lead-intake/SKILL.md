# Lead Intake — the two minutes that decide whether a lead survives

Leads die in the gap between the conversation and the CRM. This skill closes it in one
pass and makes sure the next action has a date on it.

Writes into `data/contacts.csv` and `data/pipeline.csv`.

---

## Capture

Ask for what is missing, in one message, and accept partial answers. A lead with a name
and a phone number is worth logging; do not block on completeness.

**Identity:** first and last name, mobile, email.

**Source — the field agents skip and the one that matters most.** Where exactly did this
come from: a referral (from whom, by name), an open house (which one, what date), a sign
call, a portal, a social post, a past client, a walk-in. Source drives the cadence, the
tone, and the consent basis. "Online" is not a source.

**Consent basis, recorded now:**
- `express-written` — they filled in a form, signed in, or opted in. Note where.
- `existing-relationship` — a referral from a past client, or a prior client themselves
- `inbound-inquiry` — they called or messaged the agent first about a specific property
- `unknown` — anything else. Excluded from every automated send in this library.

**What they said they want:** buying or selling or both, timeframe in their own words,
area, price range, whether financing is arranged, whether they are already under a
representation agreement with another agent.

**The representation question is not optional.** If they are already under an exclusive
agreement with another agent, log it, do not pursue, and tell the agent why in one line.

## Score it, honestly

Three axes, and say the reasoning out loud:

- **Timeframe** — what they actually said, not what the agent hopes
- **Motivation** — is there a reason they must move (job, lease ending, family change,
  a property already listed) or is this exploratory
- **Readiness** — financing, a home to sell first, an agreement with someone else

Then say plainly: this is a this-month lead, a this-quarter lead, or a this-year lead.
Agents lose money treating year-out leads like this-month leads and vice versa.

**Do not score on where they live, what they look like they can afford, their name, or
anything demographic.** Score on what they said. This is both a fair housing rule and
simply a better predictor.

## The next action

Every lead exits this skill with **one next action and a date.** Not "follow up soon."
A specific thing on a specific day, written into `data/pipeline.csv`:

```
contact_id,stage,next_action,next_action_date,owner,notes,created,last_touch
```

If the right next action is a call today, say so and say it plainly rather than drafting
a text because a text is easier to produce.

## Then

- Merge into `data/contacts.csv` using the `contact-import` schema, deduplicating against
  what is already there. A lead who is already a past client is a completely different
  conversation, and finding that out at intake matters.
- Hand off to `followup-sequence` to build the cadence for this lead type.
- Draft the first touch via `sphere-message`, held for approval.

## Chains into

`followup-sequence`, `followup-queue`, `sphere-message`, `meeting-brief`.
