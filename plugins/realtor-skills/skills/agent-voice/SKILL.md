# Agent Voice — make the output sound like the agent, not like AI

The single fastest way this whole system loses an agent's trust is copy that sounds
generated. This skill fixes that once and then gets applied everywhere.

---

## Part 1 — Capture (run once, refresh occasionally)

File: `profile/VOICE.md`. If it exists, read and edit in place.

**Collect real samples, not descriptions.** Ask the agent to paste, from their own sent
folder and message history:

- 5 to 10 texts they actually sent to clients
- 3 emails: one to a new lead, one to a client mid-transaction, one to their sphere
- 2 or 3 social captions they wrote themselves
- One message they are proud of and one they think was clumsy

If they have nothing to paste, ask them to voice-record two minutes answering "how would
you tell a past client about a listing you just took?" and transcribe that. Spoken voice
is closer to their real register than anything they would write on request.

**Then extract, and write down what you actually observed:**

- Greeting patterns. Do they open with a name? A day reference? Nothing at all?
- Closing patterns. Full sign-off, first initial, nothing?
- Sentence length and paragraph spacing. Blank lines between sentences, or dense blocks?
- Contractions and shortcuts they genuinely use
- Emoji: how many, which ones, where. Some agents live on them. Record the truth.
- Punctuation habits: em-dashes, ellipses, exclamation points, ALL CAPS for emphasis
- Words and phrases that recur — their actual vocabulary
- Register shifts: how they write to a past client vs a cold lead vs another agent

**And record the bans.** Ask directly: what words make you cringe? Every agent has them.
Keep a `## Banned words` list and scan every finished draft against it before delivering —
do not just try to avoid them while writing.

## Part 2 — Apply

Any skill in this library that drafts outbound text loads `profile/VOICE.md` first.

**Universal rules, regardless of what the voice file says:**

- No filler hedges. "Honestly", "to be honest", "I just wanted to reach out", "I hope
  this email finds you well", "in today's market" all read as machine-written.
- No manufactured urgency the agent has not actually earned.
- No claim about the agent's life, history, or experience that is not in
  `profile/AGENT.md` or was not said in this session. If a line implies where they live,
  what they have personally seen, or how long they have done something, source it or
  rewrite it true.
- No promise made on the agent's behalf that they have not committed to.
- Match their emoji and punctuation level to the file, not to your defaults.

**Then run the check before you hand anything over:**

1. Read the draft against 2 or 3 of the captured samples side by side. Does it belong in
   the same set?
2. Scan for banned words.
3. Scan for claims that need a source (numbers, dates, credentials) — those route to
   `source-check`.
4. Anything public-facing about a property or a neighborhood routes to
   `compliance-check` before it ships.

## Part 3 — Keep it honest

When the agent rewrites something you drafted, that rewrite is the highest-value signal in
this whole system. Append it to `profile/VOICE.md` under `## Corrections`, with both the
version you wrote and the version they sent, dated. Three of those teach more than the
original capture did.

## Self-improvement loop

Before ending a run of this skill, review the run:

1. Did any step fail, stall, or need a workaround you had to invent?
2. Did the user correct, reject, or rewrite something meaningful in the output?
3. Did you discover something a future run would want to know (a path that moved, a
   tool that replaced another, a preference they stated out loud)?

If yes to any, propose a specific edit to this SKILL.md in one or two lines and ask
whether to apply it. Propose only changes that would alter a future run's behavior --
skip cosmetic rewording, and never propose more than two edits at once.

Do not edit this file without their go-ahead. If they say no, drop it and do not re-raise
the same suggestion in a later run of the same session.
