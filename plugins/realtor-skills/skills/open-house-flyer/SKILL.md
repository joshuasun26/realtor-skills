# Open House Flyer — the event piece

A listing flyer sells the house. An open house flyer sells **being there at 1pm on
Saturday**, and then converts the visitor into a contact. Different job, different
hierarchy.

Reads `listings/<slug>/LISTING.md`, `profile/AGENT.md`, and the QR target produced by
`open-house-signin`.

---

## Hierarchy on the page

The order of prominence, top to bottom:

1. **Hero photo** — full bleed across the top third
2. **Day, date, and time window** — the largest type on the page after the address.
   "SATURDAY, MARCH 8 · 1–4 PM". Spell the day out; nobody parses "3/8".
3. **Address and city**
4. **Price and the spec row** — beds / baths / interior sqft / lot sqft / year built
5. **Three or four supporting photos**
6. **Three to five sentences** built on the distinctive feature from `LISTING.md`
7. **QR code** with a short human-readable URL beneath it, and one line of why to scan:
   "scan to sign in" or "scan for the full photo set"
8. **Agent block** — headshot, name, license number, phone, email, brokerage and logo,
   Equal Housing Opportunity

Print production rules — 300 DPI, 0.25in bleed, 0.5in safe margin, export as PDF, CSS
physical units — are the same as `listing-flyer`. Follow that skill's production section
rather than reinventing it here.

## Also produce, in the same run

**Directional sign copy.** Short. An arrow direction, "OPEN HOUSE", the day and time, and
the agent's name. Nothing else — people read these at 30 mph. Ask how many signs and
which corners, and produce one per direction. Check the city's rules on sign placement and
whether the HOA permits them; some cities and most HOAs restrict both, and a confiscated
sign is a wasted Saturday.

**Neighbor invitation card.** Half-page or postcard sized. The property, the day and time,
and a genuine invitation to come see the inside. **No valuation hook** — nothing that
implies what their own home is worth or promises a number. Describe the event.

**A "just sold / just listed" version** of the same layout, for reuse afterward, so the
agent gets a second piece from one build.

## Before it ships

- **Scan the QR with a real phone from the printed proof.** Not from the screen. A QR
  that fails at the door wastes the entire capture strategy for that day.
- `compliance-check` on all copy, including the neighbor card.
- `source-check` on the specs and the price.
- Confirm the date and time are correct against the agent's calendar. A flyer with the
  wrong Saturday on it is the most expensive typo in this library.

## Output

`listings/<slug>/open-house-<date>/` containing `flyer.pdf`, `signs.pdf`,
`neighbor-card.pdf`, and the source HTML for each.

## Chains from / into

Called by `open-house-package`. Needs `open-house-signin` to have run first (for the QR
target). Reads `listing-intake`. Shares production rules with `listing-flyer`.
